What We Do

Three disciplines. One outcome: a brand that can't be forgotten.

We don't make music for brands. We build sound into brand infrastructure — the kind that creates recall, shapes emotion, and compounds in value over time.

01

Sonic Branding & Audio Identity

The highest-value work we do. Sonic logos, brand themes, audio identity systems, UI and app sounds, sound palettes for campaigns — the full sonic infrastructure that moves a brand out of "music-for-hire" territory and into long-term brand equity. Once built, it compounds.

02

Cinematic Music for Brand Campaigns

We make brands feel like cinema. Custom scores for commercials, product launches, brand films, trailers, and YouTube content — built with the emotional architecture of great film, delivered for the modern content ecosystem. This is what 1.2M people showed up for.

03

Strategic Sound Direction & Consulting

For brands that already have content but haven't thought strategically about sound. Sonic audits, music direction for campaigns, emotional positioning through audio, and cohesive sound systems across every touchpoint. Sound shapes perception — most brands leave that on the table.

The Founder

Brendan Foery

Founder & Creative Director, FCG

Brendan Foery is the founder of Foery Creative Group — a sonic branding and cinematic audio studio focused on helping brands create emotionally unforgettable experiences through sound. With a background in cinematic composition, music production, and creative direction, he specializes in building music that carries the emotional weight of film into modern brand storytelling.

Sound is not decoration — it is one of the most powerful tools a brand has to shape memory, emotion, and perception.

That belief drives everything we build. Every track, every decision is made with one question: will this make people feel something they won't forget?
