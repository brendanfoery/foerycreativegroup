The Philosophy

Sound arrives before understanding.

You've already made an emotional decision about a brand before you read a headline, before a logo registers. That decision came from somewhere — the tone of the music, the texture of the environment, the feeling the thing created. Sound sets the emotional context that everything else lands inside.

Most brands leave this entirely to chance — or to whoever picks the stock track at the end of post. I exist because there's a better way. Intentional sound doesn't just support a brand. It creates a feeling that outlasts every visual.

"When the sound is right, people don't notice it — they just feel differently about the brand."

How It Works

Brand Psychology

I start by understanding your brand from the inside — not just the positioning doc, but the feeling you're trying to create and the perception gap you actually need to close. Strategy before sound, always.
